Provenance - eco_trades_baseline.json.gz
=======================================

eco_trades_baseline.json.gz is generated, not authored. It is a per-item price
baseline built from every trade the DiscordLink bot posted to the Sirens
#eco-trades channel, for pages that want "what does this usually go for".

  Source        : Sirens Discord, #eco-trades, DiscordLink trade embeds
  Fetched       : 2026-09-27, 06:39 to 07:20 UTC, 1,403 pages
  Coverage      : 140,210 messages, 202,368 trade lines, 1,598 items, 125 currencies,
                  trades from 2024-11-09 to 2026-08-24 (the channel's last post)
  Generated by  : scripts/trades_baseline.py  (just trades-baseline fetch, then build)
  Tests         : tests/test_trades_baseline.py

What one observation is
-----------------------

Each trade embed lists "Sold" or "Bought" lines of the form
"N X Item * unit = total", plus a "Result" line naming the currency. One line is
one observation of a unit price at quantity N. "Bought" and "Sold" are from the
trading player's side, and both are pooled in every figure (`sides` counts them).
Embeds that say "No currency was exchanged" are filed under the currency Barter.
Negative unit prices are real: they are store fees for taking garbage and tailings.

What the numbers mean
---------------------

Every Eco cycle's players invent their own currencies (Souls, Spectres, Sparks,
Racines, personal "X Credit" notes), and a unit in one is not a unit in another.
So:

  byCurrency.<name>  like-for-like figures in one currency. Use these to compare.
  pooled             every currency mixed together. A rough level, not a price.

Each block carries observations, quantity, median, mean, weightedMean (by
quantity), p25, p75, min, max, firstSeen and lastSeen (UTC dates).

`itemId` is the Eco item id when the item's display name appears in
eco_autogen_data.json.gz. It is null for items that file does not carry as a
recipe product or ingredient: skill scrolls, and items added by server mods.
Match those by display name.

Checks run at generation
------------------------

  source.unparsedLines     trade lines the parser could not read
  source.totalMismatches   lines where N * unit does not reproduce the stated
                           total within display rounding
  Both were 0 and 0 for the committed file.

Reading it
----------

It is stored gzipped (493 KB rather than 3.9 MB), like eco_autogen_data.json.gz:

  gzcat data/eco_trades_baseline.json.gz | jq '.items["Lumber"].pooled'

Regenerate
----------

  just trades-baseline fetch /tmp/eco-trades.jsonl
  just trades-baseline build /tmp/eco-trades.jsonl

The raw dump carries player names and is not committed. The built file keeps
item names, prices and currency names only. Personal currencies are named after
their player, as they are in game.
